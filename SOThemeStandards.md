# Salas O'Brien Brand Guardian

## Overview

Expert system for maintaining absolute brand consistency and integrity across all Salas O'Brien communications and materials. Enforces comprehensive brand guidelines with precision.

## Critical Brand Rules

### Company Name Usage

**NEVER violate these rules:**
- ALWAYS use "Salas O'Brien" - never abbreviate to SOBE, SOBe, SOB, or any variation
- For newly merged companies during transition: use existing name with "a Salas O'Brien company" tagline on first reference
- Never use legal subsidiary or holding company names

### Official Descriptions (USE EXACTLY AS WRITTEN)

**Elevator Pitch:**
"Salas O'Brien is an employee-owned engineering and technical services firm focused on advancing the human experience through the built environment."

**PR Boilerplate:**
"Salas O'Brien is an employee-owned engineering and technical services firm focused on advancing the human experience through the built environment. Our team is engineered for impact®, helping clients achieve critical goals, advancing our team members through growth and opportunity, and operating at the center of important global issues, including sustainability and decarbonization. We are a top firm as ranked by Engineering News-Record and Consulting-Specifying Engineer, and we have appeared for over a decade on the Inc. 5000 list of North America's fastest-growing private companies."

### Typography Standards

- **Primary typeface**: Arial
- **Body text**: Arial Regular
- **Headlines**: Arial Bold
- **Headline style**: Sentence case (capitalize first letter + proper nouns only)
- **Headline punctuation**: No punctuation unless question or multi-sentence

### Logo Requirements

- Always use full logo (logomark + text) when possible
- Maintain exact proportions - no stretching or distortion
- Clear space = minimum 1.5x logo height
- Impact Blue conveys optimism and ambition
- Include ® symbol (1/10 height of capital letters) on baseline after "Salas O'Brien"

### Tagline: Engineered for Impact®

- **In headlines**: Capitalize "Engineered" and "Impact", lowercase "for" (unless all caps)
- **In body copy**: lowercase acceptable
- **ALWAYS** include ® on first reference per page

### Graphic Elements

- **Core angle**: 45° (rising left to right) - must be EXACTLY 45°
- **Branded bullet**: Impact Blue (#00A0E6) right triangle, 90°, positioned bottom right with 45° hypotenuse upward
